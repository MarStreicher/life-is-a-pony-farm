Life is a Pony Farm is a small place with a simple aim: let more people see the pony who lives here.

The pasture is the whole point. There is grass, a fence line, and a routine that repeats until it feels like weather. The pony knows the sound of the gate. Visitors who come quietly get the real version of the farm, which is mostly standing still and watching ears flick at something in the hedge.

This site is the noticeboard. Stories from the field live under [Stories](/posts/). If you want to come in person, start with [Visit](/visit/).

The photographs on the site are temporary stand-ins, borrowed so the pages have a landscape to sit in. The real pasture will replace them.
